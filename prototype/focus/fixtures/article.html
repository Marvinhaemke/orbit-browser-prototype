The space between.

The best interfaces leave room for the activity they support. A page should feel like a page. A tool should be close when you need it, and quiet when you don’t.

At the edge

Move to the top middle for an address. Touch the corner for a few useful tools. This is a small experiment in bringing controls to your attention, instead of asking your attention to live around the controls.

Try opening another page, switching back, and seeing where your place remains. The prototype keeps every tab’s document alive until you close it.

A small gesture

Hold the right mouse button and explore the tab wheel. A slice is a forgiving target: its whole area belongs to the action. The circular highlight simply tells you where you are.

You can also click an action, or use arrow keys and Enter. Escape returns through the menu’s layers.

Room to return

Frames give the things you’re thinking about a home. Tabs from the same site gather together, making a busy set of pages feel a little easier to navigate.
